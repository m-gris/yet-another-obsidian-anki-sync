## Anatomy of a long bone #flashcard/cloze

The shaft of a long bone is the ==<<diaphysis>>==, and each widened end is an
==<<epiphysis>>==. Between them sits the ==<<epiphyseal plate>>==, the cartilage layer
where lengthwise growth happens. The whole outer surface, except at the joint,
is wrapped in a fibrous membrane called the ==<<periosteum>>==.

## Cells that remodel bone #flashcard/cloze

Bone is continuously rebuilt: ==<<osteoblasts>>== deposit new matrix while
==<<osteoclasts>>== dissolve old matrix and release its calcium. An osteoblast that
becomes walled into the matrix it secreted matures into an ==<<osteocyte>>==, which
then senses mechanical load and signals where remodelling is needed.
