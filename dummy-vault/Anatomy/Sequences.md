# Sequences

One card whose answer is revealed one item at a time, on one schedule. The
marker says that the ORDER is the knowledge; the tool cannot see that from the
body, which is why it has to be written on the heading.

Note the shape below, because it is the shape that works: a lead-in line, then
the list, and NOTHING after it. Everything in the body that is not a list item
is printed on the question side, so a sentence written after the list would give
the answer away before the first reveal.

## Path of blood through the right heart #flashcard/sequence

From the body back towards the lungs:

- superior vena cava
- right atrium
- tricuspid valve
- right ventricle
- pulmonary valve
- pulmonary artery
